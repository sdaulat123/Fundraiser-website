Wraparound work is most effective when coaching, school coordination, and behavior support happen in the environments where problems actually show up.

## Real-environment support

This work is strongest when intervention happens where daily life is actually unfolding. That means home visits, school coordination, mentorship, and behavior support that can be observed and adjusted in context.
